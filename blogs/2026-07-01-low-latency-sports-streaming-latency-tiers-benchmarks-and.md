This article explains different live sports streaming latency tiers, from standard (10-30 seconds) to real-time (sub-second), and the technical factors influencing latency such as CDN delivery, player buffering, and ad insertion. It advocates tailoring latency configurations to use cases like interactive betting or large-scale broadcasts rather than always chasing the lowest possible delay.
