Dolby OptiView has launched new video-on-demand capabilities including DVR, live-to-VOD, and VOD upload features for sports streaming platforms. These tools enable viewers to rewind and catch key moments during live broadcasts, while allowing broadcasters to repurpose content for on-demand libraries and increase monetization opportunities. The additions give streaming operators a more complete toolkit for turning live events into long-lived content assets.
