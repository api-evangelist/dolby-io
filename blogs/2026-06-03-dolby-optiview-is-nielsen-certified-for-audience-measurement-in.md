Dolby OptiView has achieved Nielsen certification for audience measurement in streaming analytics, enabling standards-based real-time viewership tracking. This certification allows publishers to report accurate viewer data to advertisers and brand partners, building trust through independent verification. The milestone supports better content strategy decisions and gives streaming platforms a competitive advantage in attracting ad spend through verified audience metrics.
